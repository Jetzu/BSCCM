# N059 diagnosis red-team — ranked findings

**Localization survives; causality and reachability do not.** A simpler explanation fits: ordinary supervised transfer across two isolations, unequal training data and target transformations, plus remaining estimation/measurement error. Shared image-regressor errors do not uniquely identify antibody/unmixing differences or prove .70 reachable.

1. **The in-distribution reference cannot isolate label readout.** N039 uses both draws’ supervised images and labels, transductive n007 SSL, v5 targets, and 317,935 training cells; N048 uses inductive SSL, cofactor-150 targets, and 269,283/127,946 cells (b10/b01). Evaluation cells match, but training distributions, objectives and sample sizes do not. Re-scoring the existing 128k-cell N039 reference on the same v10 cells gives **.6646**, versus full-data **.6862** and ensemble **.6862**. Matching total count still does not match source/target composition. Full-reference-minus-ensemble positive target differences contribute +.02325 to GOAL, negative differences −.02323: equality is cancellation, not evidence of zero average draw shift. HLA/group advantages could reflect supervised adaptation of features, population relationships or assay readout; no controlled intervention separates them. Evidence: run `result.json` files, N039 item, direct NumPy rescore.

2. **“Calibration is NOT the gap” tests an almost vacuous oracle.** In `n059_diag.csv`, **19/22 scores have one slide**, including every single marker. Positive affine transforms leave Pearson unchanged; fitted affine predictions have r = |original r|. Only three b01 pooled scores can improve by aligning slides. Thus +.00286 bounds that alignment, not nonlinear or population-dependent calibration. Countercheck: a scalar piecewise-linear oracle (intercept, prediction, nine hinges at training deciles; five folds by sorted unique FOV index modulo five) scores **.6904**, versus .6862. b10 HLA-DR rises **.5256→.5905**, grp:CD45 **.7526→.7802**, grp:CD16 **.8527→.8752**. This exploratory oracle uses target-draw labels: it is not an inductive gain or proof of .70, but defeats the broad exclusion of calibration. Evidence: `n059_diag.py`, CSV, direct CPU check.

3. **The .70 bridge selectively imports oracle wins.** Replacing only HLA-DR/grp:CD45/grp:CD16 with full N039 scores adds **.01727→.70346**, rather than +.016→.702. N039 loses elsewhere, notably b10 CD123 **.6495→.3652**. This target-selected hybrid of differently trained models is not a demonstrated jointly achievable predictor. No source-only procedure has recovered these target-draw improvements. The proposed images-only falsifier would also violate GOAL’s held-out-batch SSL exclusion if used as a candidate solution; diagnostically, failure of one adaptation method cannot prove impossibility for every inductive model. Evidence: CSV arithmetic, GOAL.md, diagnosis falsifier.

4. **Weak CD19/CD56 do not establish a ceiling “for any model.”** Shared weakness supports difficult/noisy measurements, but both references use similar regression recipes and lack independent repeat measurements. K028/K033 report label-count gains (weak targets +.035 from 50→100% labels), leaving finite-data error plausible; N039’s v5 objective is especially mismatched for dim markers under v10. Demeaning both labels and predictions within FOV changes the estimand, removing genuine composition differences as well as measurement offsets: .726 is not automatically attainable pooled r. Background analyses independently support label error, but do not quantify all remaining error. Evidence: K028–K034, `stats()`, CSV.

5. **Metric and attempt history overstate specificity.** Pearson .686 versus Spearman .531 does not locate poor ranking within negatives without a negative-stratum analysis. HLA-DR has **Spearman > Pearson in both directions** (.627>.526; .510>.440), consistent with amplitude distortion rather than universal ranking failure. N054/N056/N057 are void for this GOAL; only N055 directly follows N053 and its registered ensemble improves **.6862→.6902**, below the chosen significance bar. This is a modest/inconclusive gain, not demonstrated saturation or a uniquely label-side explanation. Evidence: CSV, attempts.md, K039/K040.

Checks used existing predictions and NumPy only; ensemble cell ordering matched and N039 coverage was complete. No training or repository changes. Draw-specific readout remains plausible, but is not distinguished from the simpler transfer/data/objective explanation.
