### Why the simpler account survives

- **Source coverage has a measured effect with the destination fixed.** N082 had 127,946 training rows for b01 and 269,283 for b10. Doubling distinct training rows at matched optimizer steps moved b01 from .6414 to .6592 (+.0178) and b10 from .6736 to .6867 (+.0131). The b01 change is comparable to E8’s .01969 direction gap. This was one ResNet seed. It demonstrates a competing mechanism without estimating its contribution to E8. The per-target count/asymmetry correlation of .18 also cautions against attributing the entire gap to sample count. [N082 design and results](../../../notebook/items/N082-step-matched-learning-curve-50-training.md#L12) · [Count check](../../../notebook/KNOWLEDGE.md#L15)
- **Only two of the five attempts tested an E8 variant.** N087’s decoder comparison used a frozen ResNet. N090 tested two smaller encoder learning rates and dropped stage 2. N093 scored Giemsa clinical transfer. N091 and N094 provide direct evidence against adding that DINO member and averaging those seeds. Moreover, the N094 “independent full re-draw” reuses the original base composite and plain ConvNeXt predictions verbatim. Seed averaging still failed its registered bar. Six E8 members had already been selected using the same two evaluation draws, and the .0027 noise estimate comes from two seed pairs, not donor sampling. - **Background disagreement establishes sensitivity to assay choice.** On draw 1, local-background and v10 labels correlate only .68 for pooled CD45 and .76 for HLA-DR. For E6, the consensus score .6996 exceeds the noise control .6722. A constructed alternate label biased toward the predictor reproduces .6996 even if v10 is exact. Field clustering of `label − prediction` can likewise include omitted morphology shared by nearby cells. N066’s failed cross-draw correction tested field density, position, focus and the prediction. It could not exclude missing information in the LF pixels. - **A correlation sign flip is compatible with perfect image prediction.** Suppose two independent visible features are U and V. Let the marker equal U + V and autofluorescence equal U − V. Their covariance is Var(U) − Var(V). Changing the population variances flips its sign while the same two readouts stay exact. This counterexample applies to the measured CD3/autofluorescence flip (−.312 to +.338). CD45/autofluorescence stays positive (.090 to .595). Autofluorescence carries zero GOAL weight. Different clip-floor fractions likewise describe marginals. Neither observation identifies an unpredictable change conditional on LF.
