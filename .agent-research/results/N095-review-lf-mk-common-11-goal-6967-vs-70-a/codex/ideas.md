### 1. Spend the same label budget across more fields

**Contradicts cell-uniform training.** Within each measured assay, give each field equal total loss weight. At a fixed sampling budget, visit distinct fields before drawing another cell from a dense field. At full budget, use all cells and weight each by the inverse of its field’s measured-cell count. If much of a field’s label error is shared, extra cells from that field add less independent supervision. Draw-0 pooled CD45 has only 1,741 equally weighted fields’ worth of weight under cell sampling, calculated as (Σn)²/Σn² from the counts above. N061’s within-field pair batches deliberately put neighbors together. [Residual clustering](../../N066-review-4-lf-mk-v10-goal-6862-target-70-a/n066_fovresid.csv#L22) · [Prior sampler control](../../../notebook/items/N061-within-fov-pairwise-difference-loss-shar.md#L15)

**Falsifier and build.** At half the source label budget, compare wide field coverage with cell-uniform sampling, matching steps, assay counts and marker-quantile histograms. Validate on the same untouched source fields. Kill if wide coverage fails to improve pooled targets or the gain disappears at full budget. First paired ResNet screen **0.7–1.2 GPU-h**, **one day** for sampler and matched controls. Forecast **0–.004**, equivalent to recovering up to half the b01 gain from doubling distinct source rows (+.0178), then averaging directions. ### 2. Let single-marker predictions supervise the pooled residual

Learn a strongly shrunk linear correction to the pooled head’s residual from the single outputs. Distill that correction into the end-to-end pooled predictor on disjoint source fields, with measured pooled v10 as the anchor. No lineage assignments or new target definitions are needed. ### 4. Protect marker updates from conflicting auxiliary gradients

The standard recipe adds classification and regression losses. Gate labels are available only on draw-0 pooled cells, so auxiliary supervision is directionally asymmetric. When the gate gradient opposes the gradient of measured marker loss on an independent source-field minibatch, remove just its opposing component from the shared encoder update. Keep all measured single and pooled targets. This addresses a possible **training-side** explanation for b01 weakness. **Falsifier and build.** Estimate gradient dot products twice with independent source minibatches. Stop if conflicts fail to repeat. Otherwise compare projection with standard training and with a gate-weight control matched for auxiliary gradient norm. **≤0.2 GPU-h** for the gradient screen, **one to two days**. Forecast **0–.003** if protecting b01 recovers .02 on three targets. N062’s .014–.031 pooled gains alongside single-marker losses motivate the scale. ### 5. Teach LF morphology from the paired Wright images

**Reverse the LF-to-Giemsa teacher direction.** Extract continuous nucleus area, lobulation and texture measurements from registered source Wright images. Teach an LF encoder these measurements as auxiliary tasks on paired cells while training markers on separate stained cells. A morphology readout that generalizes across stains could supply within-type variation missed by type-mean prediction. Protect existing Giemsa evaluation pairs, split by field, and discard the morphology heads at deployment.
